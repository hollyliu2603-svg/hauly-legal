Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Plain-English Summary

Hauly is an organising app, not a shopping or advertising platform. We collect only what is needed to save your collection and let you connect with friends. We do not sell your data, show you ads, or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Who we are

Hauly is a mobile app for organising your personal beauty collection. This policy describes how we handle your information when you use the app.

If you have questions, email us at haulyapp@outlook.com.

What we collect

Account information

  • Your email address — used only for sign-in and account recovery.
  • A username you choose — displayed instead of your real name everywhere in the app.

Your collection

  • Product names, brands, categories, shades, finishes, and notes you enter.
  • Opened dates and status (sealed, in use, almost finished, etc.).
  • Photos you attach to products — stored securely in your account.

Social features (Circle)

  • Friend connections — stored as pairs of usernames. No real names are linked.
  • Posts and comments you write in the Circle tab.
  • Likes you give to friends' posts.

What we do not collect

🚫  Your real name

🚫  Your location or GPS data

🚫  Payment information (the app is free; there are no purchases)

🚫  Your phone number

🚫  Any data for advertising or profiling purposes

How we use your information

  • To save your collection and sync it across your devices.
  • To display your collection and expiry dates back to you.
  • To send you local notifications about expiring products and restock reminders — these are scheduled on your device and never leave it.
  • To show Circle posts and comments to your accepted friends.
  • To let you search for and connect with friends by username.

We do not use your data to train machine-learning models, build advertising profiles, or sell insights to third parties.

Third-party services

We use the following infrastructure providers to operate the app:

Supabase — provides our database, authentication, and file storage. Your collection data and photos are stored on Supabase servers. Supabase is compliant with GDPR and stores data in the EU and US regions. See Supabase's privacy policy.

Open Beauty Facts — an open, community-maintained product database used for barcode lookups. When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Beauty Facts to retrieve product information. No personal information is sent. See Open Beauty Facts' privacy policy.

No other third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries are included in the app.

Your photos

If you attach a photo to a product, it is stored in your private account storage on Supabase. Photos are only accessible by you. Friends cannot view your product photos through the Circle feed — only colour swatches derived from what you enter are shared.

Photos are compressed and resized before upload to reduce storage. The originals are not kept.

Local notifications

Hauly can send you reminders about expiring products and items that are almost finished. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device using iOS's local notification system. No data is sent to our servers to deliver them. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Profile tab or in iOS Settings.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. If you delete a product, it is removed from our database. If you delete your account, all associated data — including your collection, photos, posts, and friend connections — is permanently deleted within 30 days.

To delete your account, contact us at haulyapp@outlook.com. We will confirm deletion by email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data ("right to erasure").
  • Export a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Opt out of any direct marketing (we send none, but this right exists).

To exercise any of these rights, email haulyapp@outlook.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Australian users: if you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

UK / EU users: you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (ICO in the UK; your national DPA in the EU).

Automated processing

When you use the barcode scanner, the barcode number is sent to Open Beauty Facts to retrieve product information automatically. No personal information is transmitted. No decisions that affect your rights or access to the app are made automatically — all results are presented for you to review and confirm before anything is saved.

Children's privacy

Hauly is not directed at children under 15. Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (as amended by the Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024), we treat users under 15 as children and do not knowingly collect their personal information without verified parental consent. If you believe a child under 15 has created an account, please contact us at haulyapp@outlook.com and we will delete the account and all associated data promptly.

Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are never stored — authentication uses a secure one-time PIN sent to your email. Photos and collection data are stored in access-controlled storage. While no system is perfectly secure, we take reasonable technical measures to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For significant changes, we will notify you via the app or email.
